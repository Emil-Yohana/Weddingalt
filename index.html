How It All Began

Our Story

❧

Our story began on August 9, 2025, with a simple connection on an app, two people living on opposite sides of the world, not knowing just how much their lives were about to change.


After months of getting to know each other across the distance, we finally met in person on December 18, 2025, in England. What followed was a beautiful adventure filled with unforgettable moments, from the streets of Paris to Portugal and Rome. Somewhere between the long conversations, the flights, the laughter, and the goodbyes, we found something worth crossing the world for.


Now, our next adventure begins in New Zealand, where we can't wait to celebrate our love and the beginning of our marriage with the people who mean the most to us.
